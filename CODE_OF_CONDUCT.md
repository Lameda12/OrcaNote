# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in OrcaNotes to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Lameda12) or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Lameda12/OrcaNote/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially.
