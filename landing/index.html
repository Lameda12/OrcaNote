Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

How does audio recording work without a bot?

OrcaNotes uses Apple's native ScreenCaptureKit and AVFoundation to capture both your microphone and your computer's system audio (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or browser) directly at the OS level.

Do I need a Groq API key?

Yes. Groq provides ultra-fast Whisper transcription and Llama 3.3 summarization for a fraction of a cent per meeting (often free on their generous tier). You just paste your key in .env once.

What macOS versions are supported?

macOS Sonoma (14.0+), macOS Sequoia (15.0+), and future macOS versions on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
